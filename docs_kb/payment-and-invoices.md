# Payment and invoices

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, bank transfer and, for business accounts, payment by invoice. Mobile money (Orange Money, Wave) is accepted for deliveries in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal.

## Paying by invoice
Business accounts can pay by invoice after a credit check. The invoice is due 30 days after the delivery date. The first order paid by invoice is limited to 5,000 EUR excluding tax; the limit can be raised after three orders paid on time.

## Getting an invoice
Invoices are emailed when the order ships and can be downloaded from "My orders" as PDF. To change the company name, address or VAT number on an invoice, contact support within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Late payment
A reminder is sent 7 days after the due date. After 30 days, new orders paid by invoice are blocked until the balance is paid, and a late-payment fee of 40 EUR applies as required by French law.

## Currency and taxes
Prices are shown in EUR. For Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal, prices are converted to XOF at checkout and local import taxes are included in the price shown.
