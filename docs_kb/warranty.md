# Warranty and after-sales service

## Warranty length
All products have a 2-year warranty from the delivery date. Office chairs and standing desks have an extended 5-year warranty on the frame and the lifting mechanism. Batteries and consumables are covered for 6 months.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, wear of fabric and padding, damage caused by liquid, or products modified or repaired by someone else.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number, a description of the problem and a photo or short video. A technician answers within 2 business days. If the product cannot be fixed remotely, we send a replacement part, repair the product, or replace it.

## Repairs and spare parts
Spare parts for chairs and desks (wheels, armrests, gas lifts, controllers) are available for 7 years after the product is discontinued. Parts covered by the warranty are free, including shipping.
