# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery areas
Kora Office Supplies delivers to France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal. Orders to other countries are not accepted online; contact the sales team for a quote.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days in France and Belgium, and 5 to 8 business days in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal. Express delivery (France only) arrives the next business day if the order is placed before 1 pm.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery is free for orders above 150 EUR excluding tax. Below that amount, standard delivery costs 9.90 EUR. Express delivery costs 19.90 EUR whatever the order amount. Furniture items (desks, chairs, cabinets) have a flat delivery fee of 49 EUR, which includes installation in the room of your choice.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is sent by email as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. You can also follow every order from the "My orders" page of your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.

## Damaged or missing parcels
If a parcel arrives damaged, write a note on the carrier's delivery slip and send us photos within 48 hours. If a parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the latest expected date, contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier. A replacement is shipped as soon as the investigation confirms the loss.
