# Account and orders

## Creating a business account
Click "Create an account" and choose "Business". You need your company name, registration number and VAT number. Business accounts get volume discounts, several users per company and payment by invoice.

## Adding colleagues
The account owner can invite colleagues from "Company settings > Users". Each user can have the role Buyer (places orders), Approver (validates orders above a limit) or Viewer (sees orders and invoices only).

## Order approval
Companies can require approval for orders above an amount they choose. The order stays "Waiting for approval" until an Approver accepts it; it is cancelled automatically after 7 days without a decision.

## Changing or cancelling an order
An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge until it is prepared, usually within 2 hours after it is placed. After that, cancel the delivery by refusing the parcel or use the normal return process.

## Resetting a password
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. For security reasons, support cannot see or send your password.
