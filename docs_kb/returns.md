# Returns and refunds

## Return period
You can return any item within 30 days of delivery, for any reason. Furniture that has been assembled and printer consumables that have been opened (ink, toner) cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

## How to return an item
Open the order in "My orders", click "Return an item", choose the items and the reason, then print the prepaid return label. Pack the item in its original packaging if possible and drop it at any partner relay point. Pickup at your office can be booked for furniture.

## Return shipping costs
Returns are free when the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered. For other reasons (change of mind, wrong size), a fee of 6.90 EUR is deducted from the refund. Furniture pickup for a change of mind costs 39 EUR.

## Refund timing
The refund is issued within 5 business days after the warehouse receives and checks the item. It is paid with the original payment method. Bank transfers can take 2 to 3 more days to appear on your account. Orders paid by invoice receive a credit note instead.

## Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order; this is the fastest way to get the right product.
